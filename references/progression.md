# Hoenn story progression

Distilled from the Prima Official Game Guide for vanilla Pokémon Emerald (2005), walkthrough
chapters only. It covers where to go, what to do, what blocks the way and where HMs, bikes and
key items come from. Pokémon, moves, wild encounters and trainer or gym teams are left out on
purpose, because EK changes all of them. Items and TMs are as in vanilla Emerald. EK moves a few,
and the EK Mastersheet notes printed under each place win. Lines tagged [Emerald knowledge] fill
gaps where the scan was unreadable; they come from general vanilla Emerald knowledge, not the
guide.

## Quick reference: HMs, bikes and key items

- HM01 Cut: Cutter's House, Rustboro City, on your first visit. Usable outside battle after the Stone Badge.
- HM02 Fly: your rival on Route 119, after the Weather Institute. Feather Badge.
- HM03 Surf: Wally's father, Petalburg City, after you beat Norman. Balance Badge.
- HM04 Strength: Rusturf Tunnel (in the guide's item table; the text doesn't say who gives it). Heat Badge.
- HM05 Flash: the Hiker at the Granite Cave entrance [Emerald knowledge]. Knuckle Badge.
- HM06 Rock Smash: the house right of the Poké Mart, Mauville City. Dynamo Badge.
- HM07 Waterfall: Wallace, Sootopolis City, after Rayquaza calms Groudon and Kyogre [Emerald knowledge]. Rain Badge.
- HM08 Dive: Steven, Mossdeep City, after the Space Center battle. Mind Badge.
- Mach Bike or Acro Bike: Rydel's Cycles, Mauville City, free, and you can swap any time. The Mach Bike crosses cracked floors and climbs sand slopes. The Acro Bike hops rails and climbs back up Jagged Pass.
- PokéNav, with the Hoenn map: Devon President, Rustboro City, after you return the Devon Goods.
- Old Rod: the fisherman on Dewford beach. Good Rod: the fisherman on Route 118 (needs Surf). Super Rod: the fisherman next to the Mossdeep Space Center.
- Itemfinder: your rival, after the Route 110 battle.
- Wailmer Pail: the flower shop on Route 104 (north). Soot Sack: the Glass Workshop on Route 113.
- Go-Goggles (needed for the Route 111 desert): your rival, outside Lavaridge Gym after the Heat Badge.
- Devon Scope (shows invisible Kecleon, needed for Fortree Gym): Steven, on the Route 120 bridge.
- Magma Emblem (opens the Magma Hideout on Jagged Pass): the old couple at the Mt. Pyre summit.
- Basement Key (New Mauville): Wattson in Mauville, once you have Surf [Emerald knowledge].
- Exp. Share: Devon President, after you deliver the Letter to Steven.
- S.S. Ticket: your parents, at home in Littleroot, after the Elite Four.
- Master Ball: the Team Aqua leader's room in the Lilycove hideout. A Voltorb disguised as a Poké Ball sits next to it.

## Badges 0 → 1: Littleroot to Rustboro Gym

### Littleroot Town
- **Do:** Unpack and set the clock upstairs, then meet Birch's kid next door. Head north, save Prof. Birch from the Zigzagoon on Route 101 by picking a starter from his bag, and keep it.
- **Do:** After you beat Birch's kid on Route 103, Birch gives you the Pokédex and the kid gives you 5 Poké Balls.
- **Get:** Running Shoes from Mom.

### Route 101, Oldale Town and Route 103
- **Do:** You can't catch anything until you have Poké Balls. Oldale's west exit (Route 102) is blocked by a sketching researcher until you beat Birch's kid on Route 103.
- **Navigate:** Later, with Surf, the water east of Route 103 is a shortcut to Route 110 (Mauville and Slateport).

### Route 102 and Petalburg City
- **Do:** The first trainers are on Route 102. In Petalburg, visit Norman's gym (he won't battle until you have 4 badges), then help Wally catch his first Pokémon on Route 102.
- **Navigate:** Come back with 4 badges for Norman (see Badges 4 → 5).

### Route 104 and Petalburg Woods
- **Do:** Walk north through Petalburg Woods. A Team Aqua grunt is bullying a Devon researcher; beat him (you get a Great Ball).
- **Do:** Past the woods, the twins on the bridge force your first double battle.
- **Get:** Wailmer Pail (flower shop women). TM09 Bullet Seed (young man outside the flower shop).
- **Navigate:** Mr. Briney's cottage (south shore) is empty for now. His boat opens up after the Rusturf Tunnel events. Part of the woods needs Cut.

### Rustboro City
- **Do:** Get HM01 Cut at Cutter's House, the Quick Claw from the Trainers' School teacher, then beat Roxanne. Her gym is straight hallways.
- **Get:** Stone Badge (Cut usable outside battle), TM39 Rock Tomb.

## Badges 1 → 2: Rusturf Tunnel, Dewford and Granite Cave

### Rustboro City, after the Stone Badge
- **Do:** Team Aqua robs the Devon Corporation. Chase the thief east onto Route 116 and into Rusturf Tunnel.
- **Do:** Bring the Devon Goods back. The President gives you the PokéNav and two errands: a Letter for Steven in Dewford, and the Goods for Capt. Stern in Slateport. Both need Mr. Briney's boat.
- **Get:** PokéNav (with map). Exp. Share later, when you return after delivering the Letter.
- **Navigate:** Later, bring a Mirage Tower fossil to the scientist on the 2nd floor to revive it.

### Route 116 and Rusturf Tunnel
- **Do:** Beat the Aqua grunt in Rusturf Tunnel. He hands back the Devon Goods and Mr. Briney's Peeko.
- **Get:** Devon Goods. HM04 Strength is listed in the tunnel's items.
- **Navigate:** Boulders block the tunnel's far end until you have Rock Smash and the Dynamo Badge. Clearing them later links Rustboro and Verdanturf and gets you TM45 Attract from Wanda. The east side of Route 116 (Blackglasses, found with the Itemfinder) is only reachable from Verdanturf.

### Mr. Briney's boat (Route 104) and Dewford Town
- **Do:** With Peeko returned, Mr. Briney sails you to Dewford, and later on to Slateport.
- **Do:** Dewford Gym is dark. Each trainer you beat lights more of it. Beat Brawly.
- **Get:** Old Rod (fisherman on the beach), Silk Scarf (house by the dock), Knuckle Badge (Flash usable outside battle), TM08 Bulk Up.
- **Navigate:** The Island Cave on Route 105 is a later Regi puzzle (see the post-game section).

### Granite Cave
- **Do:** Find Steven at the back of the cave and give him the Letter (you get TM47 Steel Wing). Then go back to the Devon President for the Exp. Share.
- **Get:** HM05 Flash from the Hiker at the entrance [Emerald knowledge]. Everstone (boulder on a ledge).
- **Navigate:** It's dark without Flash. The EK Mastersheet notes give the B1F and B2F ice-floor solutions.

## Badges 2 → 3: Slateport to Mauville

### Slateport City
- **Do:** Capt. Stern isn't at the Shipyard. Go to the Oceanic Museum (the Aqua queue clears once you've checked the Shipyard). Find Stern upstairs, beat the Aqua grunts who try to grab the Goods, and hand them over.
- **Get:** A gift from a grunt you met before, inside the Museum (the scan can't be read; TM46 Thief [Emerald knowledge]). Soothe Bell (Fan Club, if your lead is friendly). Pokéblock Case (Fan Club).
- **Navigate:** Later: bring the Scanner from the Abandoned Ship to Stern for a Deep Sea Tooth or Scale. After the Magma Hideout, Team Aqua steals Stern's submarine here (see Badges 6 → 7).

### Route 110
- **Do:** Walk north. Your rival blocks the way to Mauville; beat them for the Itemfinder.
- **Navigate:** Cycling Road above the route needs a bike. The Trick House (8 puzzle rooms, a prize each) is optional. New Mauville is an island in the northeast, reached by Surf, under Cycling Road.

### Mauville City
- **Do:** Get HM06 Rock Smash (house right of the Mart) and a free bike from Rydel. Beat Wally at the gym door, then Wattson. His gym uses floor switches to open the electric barriers.
- **Get:** Dynamo Badge (Rock Smash usable outside battle), TM34 Shock Wave. Coin Case from the woman next to the Game Corner, in exchange for Harbor Mail [Emerald knowledge].

## Badges 3 → 4: Route 111 to Lavaridge

### Route 117 and Verdanturf Town (optional detour west)
- **Do:** The Day Care is on Route 117. In Verdanturf, use Rock Smash on the Rusturf Tunnel boulders to reunite Wanda with her boyfriend.
- **Get:** TM45 Attract (Wanda).

### Route 111 (north of Mauville)
- **Do:** Smash the two boulders north of Mauville (Rock Smash plus Dynamo Badge). The Winstrate family house is first: beat all four in a row for the Macho Brace.
- **Do:** A sandstorm blocks the desert for now. Continue north to Route 112. Team Magma grunts are blocking the Cable Car, and you overhear them talk about Fallarbor.
- **Get:** TM43 Secret Power (man by the big tree on Route 111 or 112).
- **Navigate:** The desert needs the Go-Goggles (Badge 4). Mirage Tower and the Desert Ruins are there.

### Fiery Path and Route 113
- **Do:** Go through the Fiery Path to Route 113. At the Glass Workshop, collect the Soot Sack; it gathers ash as you walk through the ash grass.
- **Navigate:** Half of the Fiery Path is behind Strength boulders (TM06 Toxic is back there). Come back with Strength and the Heat Badge.

### Fallarbor Town
- **Do:** Team Magma has kidnapped Prof. Cozmo and taken him to Meteor Falls. Meet Lanette (the PC designer) at the Pokémon Center.
- **Navigate:** The Move Relearner here re-teaches a forgotten move for one Heart Scale. Once you have the Meteorite, return it to Cozmo for TM27 Return.

### Route 114 and Meteor Falls
- **Do:** Visit Lanette's house on Route 114 (optional). Then enter Meteor Falls: Team Magma takes the Meteorite and heads for Mt. Chimney. Talk to Prof. Cozmo.
- **Get:** TM05 Roar (man on the road).
- **Navigate:** Meteor Falls' lower levels (TM02 Dragon Claw, TM23 Iron Tail) need Waterfall. Come back late.

### Mt. Chimney (Cable Car from Route 112)
- **Do:** The Cable Car is open now. At the summit, Magma and Aqua are fighting; push north to Maxie and beat him before he throws the Meteorite into the crater. You get the Meteorite.
- **Navigate:** Walking down via Jagged Pass is one-way unless you have the Acro Bike.

### Jagged Pass and Lavaridge Town
- **Do:** Walk down Jagged Pass to Lavaridge. A woman at the hot springs gives you an Egg. In the gym, use the floor holes to drop between rooms and reach Flannery.
- **Get:** Heat Badge (Strength usable outside battle), TM50 Overheat. Go-Goggles from your rival outside the gym.
- **Navigate:** The Magma Hideout door on Jagged Pass only appears once you hold the Magma Emblem (Badges 6 → 7).

## Badges 4 → 5: Norman and Surf

### Petalburg City (return)
- **Do:** With 4 badges, challenge Norman. Each gym room has one trainer and a sign; you only need 3 rooms to reach him. Then visit Wally's house next door for Surf.
- **Get:** Balance Badge (Surf usable outside battle), TM42 Facade, HM03 Surf from Wally's father.
- **Do:** Then call in at home in Littleroot: Mom gives you the Amulet Coin [Emerald knowledge; the EK Mastersheet lists it straight after Norman too].

### Optional things that open now
- **New Mauville:** Wattson gives you the Basement Key [Emerald knowledge]. Surf to the island in the northeast of Route 110. Step on the same-coloured floor switch to open each coloured door, then hit the red switch at the generator. Wattson rewards you with TM24 Thunderbolt [Emerald knowledge].
- **Route 111 desert and Mirage Tower:** with the Go-Goggles, climb the tower (Rock Smash and Mach Bike) and take ONE fossil: Claw (Anorith) or Root (Lileep). The tower then sinks. Devon revives the fossil.
- **Fiery Path:** use Strength on the boulders for TM06 Toxic.

## Badges 5 → 6: Route 118 to Fortree

### Route 118
- **Do:** Surf across the channel east of Mauville. The fisherman on the far bank gives you the Good Rod.

### Route 119 and the Weather Institute
- **Do:** Route 119 is tall grass, so no biking. Team Aqua has taken over the Weather Institute; clear it out.
- **Do:** Your rival then battles you on the way to Fortree and gives you HM02 Fly.
- **Get:** Castform (Institute scientist), HM02 Fly.
- **Navigate:** Some trainers hide behind camouflage sheets. A rock or tree that doesn't match its neighbours is a trainer.

### Fortree City
- **Do:** An invisible Kecleon blocks the gym. Go east to Route 120 for the Devon Scope, then come back. The gym has rotating gates; push them in the right order. Beat Winona.
- **Get:** Feather Badge (Fly usable outside battle), TM40 Aerial Ace. TM10 Hidden Power (guess the coin hand three times).

### Route 120
- **Do:** Steven, on the bridge, gives you the Devon Scope. Use it on the Kecleon outside the cave at the top of the route for TM11 Sunny Day.
- **Navigate:** The Ancient Tomb (Registeel) is here, but it's sealed until the Sealed Chamber puzzle.

## Badges 6 → 7: Lilycove, Mt. Pyre and the hideouts

### Route 121, Safari Zone and Lilycove City
- **Do:** On Route 121 you see Team Aqua heading for Mt. Pyre. In Lilycove, your rival battles you in the Department Store.
- **Navigate:** Team Aqua's hideout is the sea cave east of Lilycove, and their Wailmer block Route 124 for now. The Safari Zone (Route 121) is optional. The Lilycove Move Deleter removes HM moves.

### Mt. Pyre (Surf south from Route 122)
- **Do:** Either explore the six inside floors for items or go straight outside and climb to the summit. Beat Team Aqua there. Archie and Maxie take the Red and Blue Orbs.
- **Get:** Magma Emblem from the old couple at the summit.

### Magma Hideout (Jagged Pass)
- **Do:** Walk down Jagged Pass until the ground rumbles. The Emblem reveals the door. Fight through to Maxie at the bottom [Emerald knowledge; the scan skips this].

### Slateport City and the Team Aqua Hideout (Lilycove)
- **Do:** Back in Slateport, Team Aqua steals Capt. Stern's submarine. Chase them to their hideout east of Lilycove (Surf in).
- **Do:** Warp pads link the rooms. You're held up just long enough for the sub to leave.
- **Get:** Master Ball, in the leader's room, beside a Voltorb disguised as a Poké Ball.

### Route 123 (optional)
- **Get:** TM19 Giga Drain from the woman who loves Grass types (needs a Grass type in your party).

### Route 124 and Mossdeep City
- **Do:** With the hideout cleared, Surf east to Mossdeep. Beat Tate and Liza; their gym uses warp pads and conveyor switches.
- **Do:** Team Magma raids the Space Center. Team up with Steven for a double battle. He then gives you HM08 Dive.
- **Get:** Mind Badge (Dive usable outside battle), TM04 Calm Mind, HM08 Dive, Super Rod (fisherman next to the Space Center), King's Rock (a resident).
- **Navigate:** The Treasure Hunter on a Route 124 island swaps coloured Shards (found while diving) for evolution stones. To dive, find dark water and Dive; surface at a light patch.

## Badges 7 → 8: Seafloor Cavern, Sootopolis and Sky Pillar

### Routes 126 to 128 and the Seafloor Cavern
- **Do:** Dive at Route 127 and follow the trench to the Seafloor Cavern (Route 128). Strength-boulder rooms reset if you leave and re-enter. Archie awakens Kyogre at the bottom.
- **Get:** TM26 Earthquake (in the cavern).
- **Navigate:** Needs Surf, Dive, Strength and Rock Smash. The EK notes give the room 6 and 7 solutions.

### Sootopolis City
- **Do:** Dive through the dark water on Route 126 to reach the city. Groudon and Kyogre are fighting. Steven takes you to Wallace at the Cave of Origin, who sends you to the Sky Pillar.
- **Do:** After Rayquaza ends the fight, Wallace gives you HM07 Waterfall [Emerald knowledge] and the gym opens.
- **Get:** TM31 Brick Break (a resident).

### Routes 129 to 131, Pacifidlog and the Sky Pillar
- **Do:** Surf west to the Sky Pillar. Wallace lets you in; climb to the top and wake Rayquaza. On this first visit nothing is cracked.
- **Navigate:** Pacifidlog has no Poké Mart. The Route 132–134 current is one-way west, so Fly back.

### Sootopolis Gym
- **Do:** The floor is ice: step on every tile once, never twice, to open the stairs. Stepping twice drops you to the basement. Beat Juan.
- **Get:** Rain Badge (Waterfall usable outside battle), TM03 Water Pulse.

## Badge 8 → Champion

### Ever Grande City and Victory Road
- **Do:** Waterfall up into Ever Grande (Surf and Waterfall). The Pokémon Center and Mart before Victory Road are your last stop.
- **Do:** Victory Road needs Strength and Rock Smash; Flash helps. Wally battles you at the end.

### Pokémon League
- **Do:** Sidney, Phoebe, Glacia, Drake, then Champion Wallace, back to back. After the credits you wake up in Littleroot.

## Optional and post-game

### Abandoned Ship (Route 108)
- **Do:** Surf to it. The lower decks need Dive and keys (Rm 1, 2, 4 and 6, plus the Storage Key). Find the Scanner and take it to Capt. Stern in Slateport for a Deep Sea Tooth or Scale.
- **Get:** TM13 Ice Beam and TM18 Rain Dance (likely; the scan is garbled).

### Shoal Cave (north of Mossdeep)
- **Do:** Tides decide which parts are open. Bring the man on 1F four Shoal Salts or four Shoal Shells for a Shell Bell.
- **Get:** Focus Band, TM07 Hail, Shoal Salt and Shoal Shells, Heart Scales.
- **Navigate:** The guide gives the tides as high 9am–3pm and 9pm–3am, low 3pm–9pm and 3am–9am (partly garbled).

### Sealed Chamber and the Regis
- **Do:** Dive at the dark water ringed by rocks on south Route 134 (stay south, or the current sweeps you past). Braille inside gives the steps; the second room opens all three Regi doors.
- **Navigate:** EK's door steps and what to bring are in the EK notes under the Sealed Chamber (run `area "sealed chamber"`). Regirock: Desert Ruins (Route 111). Regice: Island Cave (Route 105). Registeel: Ancient Tomb (Route 120).

### Rayquaza (Sky Pillar, second visit)
- **Do:** Once the Sootopolis crisis is over, Rayquaza is back on top of the Sky Pillar, catchable before or after the League [Emerald knowledge]. The floors are now cracked, so you need the Mach Bike. You get one chance; save first.

### Groudon, Kyogre, Latias and Latios (after the League)
- **Do:** Ask the Weather Institute researcher (Route 119) where the drought or the heavy rain is. Groudon is in the Terra Cave, on a cliff face on Route 114, 115, 116 or 118. Kyogre is in the Marine Cave: dive at dark water in the rain. Each gives one chance.
- **Do:** Latias or Latios: answer Mom's TV question after the credits. It roams in grass outdoors; track it with the PokéNav map.

### Other post-game
- **Do:** The S.S. Tidal goes from Slateport or Lilycove (it has a Leftovers). There's a new cave in Meteor Falls (Steven), and the Safari Zone gets two more areas.
- **Do:** The Desert Underpass (Fossil Maniac's house, Route 114; the scan prints 115) holds the fossil you didn't take.
- **Do:** Mirage Island (Route 130) appears only on days the Pacifidlog man can see it.
